Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-03 · NACS Lab Terminal

We process account email and password hashes (PBKDF2) on Cloudflare KV to authenticate you. Session tokens are stored in HttpOnly cookies.

Exchange API keys are encrypted in your browser (AES-GCM, passphrase-derived) and held in memory only while unlocked. They are not uploaded to NACS servers.

Optional product analytics are not enabled by default. Feed requests go to public exchange endpoints from your device.

Contact the operator of your deployment for data-subject requests (access/erasure) under applicable law (e.g. GDPR).
